FAQ

Check-in, answered.

From Bahi Hut's guest FAQ. Answers below are specific to that property; confirm before using for Golden Host.

What are the standard check-in and check-out times?

Check-in starts at 3:00 PM and check-out is at 11:00 AM. Let us know if you need anything else.

Can I request a late check-out?

We can usually extend your stay until 12:00 PM. Just text us on the morning of your check-out to confirm we have the availability.

Is early check-in available?

Yes. Early check-in is $20 per hour up to 3:00 PM. Just text us on your way in and we'll do our best to get your room ready.

Can I store my luggage at the front desk?

You sure can. Feel free to drop your bags at the front desk if you arrive early or want to explore after check-out.

How do I return my room keys when leaving?

Just leave your key cards on the desk in your room and text us back that you're heading out. Safe travels.

How do I use the lockbox for an after-hours arrival?

We'll text you a special code and instructions for the lockbox right before you arrive so you can grab your keys easily.
